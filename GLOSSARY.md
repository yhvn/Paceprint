# Paceline

An iPhone app for recording runs and sharing them as photo-backed, pace-colored route cards.

## Language

**Run**:
One recorded session from start to stop, including any pauses in between.
_Avoid_: Jog, workout, activity, session

**Route**:
The ordered path of locations a Run covered.
_Avoid_: Track, path, trace

**Split**:
The portion of a Run covering one whole unit of distance (one km or one mile).
_Avoid_: Lap, segment

**Pace**:
Time taken per unit of distance (e.g. 5:15 /km); never expressed as speed.
_Avoid_: Speed, tempo

**Pause**:
A stretch within a Run during which nothing counts toward its distance, Moving time or Route. Usually chosen by the user; the app starts one only when it can no longer record (location access lost, or the app was closed mid-Run). Never started because the runner stood still.
_Avoid_: Break, stop (Stop ends the Run)

**Moving time**:
A Run's duration with its Pauses removed; the TIME shown everywhere.
_Avoid_: Duration, elapsed time

**Elapsed time**:
A Run's duration from Start to Stop including Pauses.
_Avoid_: Total time

**Current pace**:
The Pace over the most recent stretch of Moving time during a Run in progress.
_Avoid_: Live pace, instant pace

**Average pace**:
A Run's Moving time divided by its distance.
_Avoid_: Mean pace, overall pace

**Partial split**:
The leftover distance after a Run's last whole Split.
_Avoid_: Remainder, last lap

**Pace color**:
The color on the pace gradient given to a stretch of Route, decided by how its Pace compares with the Run's Average pace.
_Avoid_: Heat, intensity

**Story card**:
The 9:16 image of one Run, made to be shared: its photo or dark map, its Route in Pace colors, and its headline numbers.
_Avoid_: Share image, export, poster

**Place name**:
The headline naming where a Run happened: a park or other landmark area, else a neighbourhood, else a city.
_Avoid_: City, title, location

**Place context**:
The wider areas around a Run's Place name, shown alongside it (e.g. "Brooklyn · New York").
_Avoid_: Neighbourhood, subtitle, address
